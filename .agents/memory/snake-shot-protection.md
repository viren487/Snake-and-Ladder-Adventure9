---
name: Snake-shot protection
description: Three-dice protection belongs to the shooting player, not the whole multiplayer round.
---

A shot snake stays inactive for the shooting player’s next three dice rolls. Other players’ rolls do not consume that protection and other players still encounter their own active snakes. Once those three protected rolls are over, normal bites resume on exact snake-head landings.

**Why:** The user requested that a shot snake remain inactive for three dice and then become active again. Keeping the existing player-specific interpretation avoids shortening that duration when online matches expand to four people.

**How to apply:** Count actual dice rolls by the shooter, including their bonus rolls. Do not count clicks to shoot/pass, mystery selections, other players’ rolls, or an entire multiplayer round as one die.

At a gun room, the shooter may spend one bullet on themself instead; this sends their panda Home while preserving quest progress and other powers. Once a shot snake is active again, a landing bite should still offer Anti-Venom before the slide.

**Why:** The user requested a self target and specifically asked that Anti-Venom remain usable when a previously shot snake becomes active and bites.

**How to apply:** Keep self as a separate single target (never combine it with snake targets). Reuse the normal snake-bite defense choice after stun expires.
